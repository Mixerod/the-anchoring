# W-17: Layer 5 Parts B and C: hygiene and retrieval honesty

## Goal

Implement `docs/PLAN-LAYER5.md` Part B (§4) and Part C (§5).

**Part B — three things that cost tokens on every call, forever.**

- Colour is chosen at the CLI boundary: plain for a pipe, for `NO_COLOR` at any value, or for
  `--no-color`; `--color` forces it back. Production previously emitted ANSI into every pipe.
- `kb brief --stats`, and a line under `kb verify --strict`, report the corpus in bytes by
  tier and by kind. Bytes are measured; the token figure is labelled an estimate and shows
  its divisor, because this tool runs no tokenizer.
- `maxBodyBytes` (default 6000) warns for an oversized entity body and never fails a build,
  which is what the new `advisory` finding flag is for.

**Part C — two places the retrieval layer was unfalsifiable.**

- `kb ask` now closes with what it did *not* return: entities searched and how many scored
  zero, hazards held back and why, retired and superseded documents excluded, and the kinds
  returned in full rather than ranked. Counts and reasons only.
- `tags:` has a checker. Shape is an error in both modes; a declared vocabulary makes an
  out-of-vocabulary tag an error; with no vocabulary, a tag used exactly once is an advisory
  warning.

## What the tag checker found on its first run

Every one of the 11 tags in this corpus was used exactly once. The field, added in Layer 4,
had produced no shared vocabulary at all, and nothing had ever said so. The tags on W-14,
W-15 and W-16 were consolidated into `layer-4`, `layer-5`, `retrieval`, `pack` and
`context-economy` in response — fixing the corpus rather than the threshold.
