# W-14: Layer 4 Part B: kb ask free-text retrieval

## Goal

Implement Layer 4 Part B (`kb ask`):
- Pure domain scoring and ranking over frontmatter (`id`, `title`, `tags`) without loading document bodies.
- Length-normalised term overlap with stopwords removed and field weights (tags x3, title x2, id x1).
- Invariants and open hazards always returned in full.
- Ranked matches for ADR, FLOW, WORK, INC with configurable limits and deterministic tie-breaking.
- Doctrine file summary section when present in `.anchor/doctrine/`.
- Loud and specific negative reporting when no ranked matches are found.
- `tags:` scalar field added to all six kinds and validated as lowercase slug lists in `kb verify`.
- `AGENTS.md` §2 updated to document `kb ask` as the cold-start retrieval move when no work item exists.
